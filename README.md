# 🚩 The BHAGWA Chronicle

### Preserving the Glory. Honoring the Warriors. Keeping History Alive.

**Swarajya Legacy** is a digital historical library dedicated to preserving and showcasing the extraordinary history of the Maratha Empire.

From the foundation of Swarajya under Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj to the great warriors, Maharajas, Mawlas, Peshwas, historic forts, legendary battles, and the cultural heritage that shaped an era — this project aims to bring our history closer to everyone.

Our purpose is not merely to remember the past, but to preserve its legacy for generations to come.

> **स्वराज्य हा माझा जन्मसिद्ध हक्क आहे आणि तो मी मिळवणारच.**

## 🏛️ What This Project Preserves

- **Historical Library** — Explore important personalities, events, battles, forts, and historical periods.
- **Interactive Timeline** — Journey through the rise and expansion of the Maratha Empire.
- **Historical Maps** — Discover the changing territories of Swarajya and the Maratha Empire across different eras.
- **Visual Archive** — Explore historical figures, forts, paintings, artifacts, and cultural heritage.
- **Warrior Profiles** — Learn about the bravery, contributions, and legacy of legendary warriors.
- **Historical Articles** — Understand the events, achievements, and significance behind our history.
- **Search & Explore** — Discover historical information through searchable and organized entries.
- **Light & Dark Themes** — A royal parchment experience and a Deccan Midnight atmosphere.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To make Maratha history accessible to everyone and preserve the legacy of our culture, traditions, historical achievements, and the courage of the great personalities who shaped our past.

This project is built with respect for history and a commitment to learning, remembrance, and cultural preservation.

## 🏹 Historical Scope

The library explores the history surrounding:

- The Deccan before Swarajya
- Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj
- Chhatrapati Sambhaji Maharaj
- Rajaram Maharaj and Maharani Tarabai
- Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj
- The rise of the Peshwas
- Bajirao I and Maratha expansion
- The later Maratha Empire and its end in 1818

## 🚩 A Legacy Beyond Time

The forts may stand in silence, but their stories still speak.

The names of our warriors may belong to history, but their courage, sacrifice, leadership, and legacy continue to inspire.
